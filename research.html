Research

Leadership and policy for equity in educational organizations.

I use quantitative, qualitative, and natural language processing methods to study school and district leaders and the organizational processes they influence and that influence them.

Dissertation Research

Leaders’ Career Trajectories

I examine leaders’ career paths and work responsibilities, the organizational conditions that shape those paths, and well-documented disparities in educators’ career trajectories.

  • Who Leaves and Who Steps In?
    National career-history microdata · mixed methods (survival analysis, sequence analysis, NLP, and interviews)
Research strand

Policy implementation and organizational decision-making

I examine how district and school leaders interpret policy, reconcile competing pressures, and translate broad goals into staffing, planning, accountability, and improvement practices. I am particularly interested in the gap between formal commitments and organizational routines.

  • Principal pipeline sustainability
    Qualitative research with Dr. Ellen Goldring and colleagues on institutionalizing sustainable human resource management systems for school leadership
  • District strategic planning
    Mixed-methods document and NLP analyses with Dr. Angela Cox of how districts define, measure, and operationalize equity goals
  • Teacher assignment as a racialized administrative practice
    Regression analyses using federal and state certification and performance data to examine how accountability pressures and community racial context relate to the distribution of certified teachers across tested and non-tested courses
  • Research-practice partnerships for equity
    I provided research and project management support for multiple RPPs across the South
Research strand

Social and political contexts of education

I study the policies, institutional logics, and political narratives that shape inclusion and belonging in school communities. Across this work, I ask how racialized systems operate through seemingly routine administrative decisions—and how research and leadership might interrupt them.

  • State school discipline policymaking
    Mixed-methods analyses with De’Ja Wood of state approaches to student behavior and school safety and how policy content is racialized
  • Political memory and conflicting identities in school closure deliberations
    Qualitative research with Tia R. Williams probing how district leaders and community members construct and contest the value of schools in public meetings
