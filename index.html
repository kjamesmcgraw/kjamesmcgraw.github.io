Education Leadership and Policy Researcher | Ph.D. Candidate, Vanderbilt University

I study school and district leaders, how they navigate political and organizational contexts, and how their decisions shape educational equity.

My research is grounded in my experience as a teacher and leader in Mississippi public schools and combines quantitative, qualitative, and natural language processing methods to understand education leadership and organizations.

Research agenda

Leadership and policy for equity in educational organizations

01

Leaders’ Career Trajectories

I examine leaders’ career paths and work responsibilities, the organizational conditions that shape those paths, and well-documented disparities in educators’ career trajectories.

02

Policy implementation and organizational decision-making

I study how leaders interpret and implement state and district policies, such as performance accountability and discipline, amid competing pressures and how their decisions disrupt or reproduce racialized inequities.

03

Social and political contexts of education

I explore how the social construction of students, schools, and communities relates to education policy, policymakers’ interactions with constituents, and belonging in schools.
